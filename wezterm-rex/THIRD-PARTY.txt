commit 18e9a404dbb5f56ac8c9e371278ea4c0ee439e25. Its original licenses and
REX-RUNE.md patch provenance are preserved. This fixes the temporary-file
medium ABI and exposes the upstream native image placement iterator.

with the original MIT attribution above.
